4 Semantic Colors

Success
#16A34A 5.9:1 AA
Use: Confirmations, "Salvo com sucesso", checkmarks
Warning
#F59E0B 3.0:1 ⚠
Use: Pending states, "Bloco quase lotado" badges

#DC2626 5.9:1 AA
Use: Form errors, destructive actions, logout
Info
#0EA5E9 4.6:1 AA
Use: Neutral update banners, informational notices

Accessibility: Never rely on color alone — every semantic state uses an icon AND color (✓ success, ⚠ warning, ✕ error, ℹ info).
